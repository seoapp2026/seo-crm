SEO CRM

SEO CRM is a private web application built for our SEO team to plan content, track organic performance, connect Google Search Console and Analytics, and run supervised AI-assisted workflows — all in one workspace.

Open SEO CRM Contact

Purpose of this application

SEO CRM exists to help authorized SEO operators manage website projects end-to-end: structure niches and pages, assign keywords, monitor indexing and internal links, import real metrics from Google APIs, and produce human-reviewed content recommendations. It is not a public consumer product — access is limited to invited team members and contractors working on our SEO accounts.

The application name shown on this page is SEO CRM, matching our Google Cloud OAuth consent screen configuration.

What you can do in SEO CRM

Project workspace

Organize projects, niches, pages, keywords, and URLs with cannibalization and orphan-page alerts.

Google integrations

Connect Search Console and Google Analytics 4 via OAuth to sync impressions, clicks, traffic, and engagement data.

Scheduled sync jobs

Background jobs pull historical Google metrics into the app for dashboards and reporting.

Supervised AI assistants

Draft and optimize content with AI — every output is reviewed by a human before use or publication.

WordPress export

Prepare approved content for manual publishing; the app never auto-publishes without review.

Performance monitoring

See which pages gain or lose organic visibility using synced Search Console and Analytics data.

Why SEO CRM requests Google OAuth access

SEO CRM uses Google OAuth 2.0 so each user can authorize access to their own Google Search Console and Google Analytics properties. We use read-only API scopes to:

  • Import Search Console performance data (impressions, clicks, CTR, average position)
  • Import Google Analytics 4 session and engagement metrics
  • Store synced data inside SEO CRM for dashboards and AI-assisted recommendations

Tokens are stored securely on our servers, used only for configured projects, and are not sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
