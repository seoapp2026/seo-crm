1. THE BIG PICTURE: HOW EVERYTHING WORKS TOGETHER (IN PLAIN ENGLISH)

Think of your business setup like a restaurant:

• THE CRM IS YOUR PRIVATE KITCHEN:
  This is where you do all the prep work in private. You research keywords, look at what competitors are doing, organize your products, and write your articles with AI. No visitors see this.

• WORDPRESS IS YOUR DINING ROOM (YOUR PUBLIC WEBSITE):
  This is where your visitors and customers arrive. They read your articles, look at comparison tables, and click on your affiliate or purchase links.

• THE "PUSH" BUTTON IS YOUR WAITER:
  When a dish (an article or page) is ready in the kitchen (CRM), you press one button, and the system delivers it neatly to your dining room (WordPress) as a private draft, ready for you to inspect before serving it to the public.

1. A Clean, Private Dashboard:

3. WHAT YOU CAN DO RIGHT NOW (Your 1-Click Superpowers)

• One-Click Connection Test:
  Click one button in the CRM to check if your WordPress site is connected and working.

• One-Click Push to WordPress:
  Select an article you created and send it straight to WordPress in seconds.

4. WHAT IT DOES NOT DO YET (And Why That Protects You)

• It Doesn't Automatically Overwrite Your WordPress Changes:
  If you edit something inside WordPress, the CRM won't mess with it. This keeps your live website safe.

• It Doesn't Publish Directly to the Public Without You Seeing It First:
  The CRM sends everything as a "Draft" (hidden from the public). You always have the final say before visitors see it.

• It Doesn't Upload Images to Your Media Library Automatically:
  The CRM creates the layout and connects images via links, but any private personal photos can be added directly inside WordPress if you prefer.

5. YOUR DAY-TO-DAY WORKFLOW: A REAL-WORLD STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH

Imagine you want to create a new page for your website. Here is exactly what you do from start to finish:

STEP 1: WHAT YOU DO (Inside the CRM)
1. Open the CRM and click on your Project (e.g., "My Coffee Website").
2. Type in the main topic or keyword (e.g., "Best Espresso Machines Under $500").
3. Click "Generate Draft".
4. The CRM's AI writes the text, creates a quick summary box, adds product comparison cards, and sets up the SEO title.
5. You read it on the screen. If you like it, you click "Push to WordPress".

STEP 2: WHAT THE CRM DOES AUTOMATICALLY (Behind the Scenes)
• It connects securely to your WordPress website.
• It creates a new page with the exact title, web address (slug), and clean formatted text.
• It automatically fills in the Rank Math SEO box with the search terms.
• It marks the page as a private "Draft" in WordPress.

STEP 3: WHAT SHOWS UP INSIDE WORDPRESS
• You open your regular WordPress Admin area (`wp-admin`).
• Go to "Pages" or "Posts".
• You will see your new page sitting at the top of the list marked as "Draft".
• When you click "Edit", you will see:
  - The main headline (H1) is in place.
  - The text is broken up into readable sections with bullet points.
  - The comparison table and buttons are styled and ready.
  - The Rank Math SEO box at the bottom is already green and filled in.

STEP 4: WHAT YOU CHECK MANUALLY BEFORE GOING LIVE
• Take 60 seconds to read through the page.
• Make sure your affiliate links (Amazon or custom links) go where you want.
• If everything looks good, click the blue "Publish" button in WordPress.
• That's it! Your page is live on the internet.

• Compatibility with AAWP & Affiliate Plugins:
