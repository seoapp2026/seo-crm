================================================================================
GOOGLE ADS — MINIMUM GUIDE: CREATE ACCOUNT + ADD PAYMENT DETAILS
SEO CRM · Keyword Planner preparation
================================================================================

WHAT YOU NEED BEFORE STARTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Google account (Gmail or Google Workspace)
  - Payment method: credit/debit card (most common), or PayPal/bank in some countries
  - Billing address that matches the billing country you will select
  - Business name and website URL (e.g. your client site)

Official signup: https://ads.google.com


PART 1 — CREATE THE GOOGLE ADS ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Start signup
  1. Open https://ads.google.com while logged into your Google account.
  2. Click "Start now" or "Sign up".

Step 2: Business info
  1. Enter business name (Google no longer allows URLs in the account name).
  2. Enter website URL.
  3. Optionally link YouTube / Google Business Profile — you can skip these.

Step 3: Campaign setup (skip if you only want Keyword Planner)
  Google pushes you to create a campaign. For CRM / Keyword Planner use:

  - Choose "Create account without a campaign" if offered, OR
  - Complete the wizard and PAUSE the campaign immediately after signup.

  You do NOT need a live campaign to have a valid Ads account.


PART 2 — ADD PAYMENT DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Billing is required before the account is fully active. You will land on
"Enter your account and payment details".

Step 4: Country and timezone (choose carefully)
  1. Billing country — where your card/bank is registered.
     - Sets currency and available payment methods.
     - Hard to change later.
  2. Time zone — affects reports and billing dates.
     - Pick the timezone you actually work in.

Step 5: Promo code (optional)
  - If you have a Google Ads coupon, enter it under "Introductory offer" → Apply.
  - Otherwise skip.

Step 6: Payment profile
  Google creates a payments profile (legal/billing identity):

  1. Profile type:
     - Organization — company / freelance with business name
     - Individual — personal name (tax treatment varies by country)
  2. Fill in:
     - Organization or legal name
     - Full address
     - Tax/VAT info if your country asks for it
  3. Click Create (or use the default profile if already correct).

Step 7: Add payment method
  1. Click "Add payment method".
  2. Enter card (or PayPal/bank if shown for your country).
  3. Google may place a small temporary hold on the card (usually removed within ~7 days).
  4. Accept Google Ads Terms and Conditions.
  5. Click Submit.

Step 8: Payment mode

  Automatic payments
    Google charges when you hit a threshold or monthly.
    Ads can run as soon as billing is verified.

  Manual payments
    You add funds yourself.
    Ads only run after you click "Add funds".

  If you do not want to spend yet: pause all campaigns and, for manual billing,
  do not add funds.


PART 3 — IF YOU ALREADY CREATED THE ACCOUNT BUT SKIPPED BILLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Go to https://ads.google.com
  2. Click the Billing icon (wallet) in the top bar
  3. Open Settings → https://ads.google.com/aw/billing/settings
  4. Under "Payment methods" → "Add payment method"
  5. Enter card details → Save → set as primary if needed


PART 4 — GET THE ID YOUR CRM NEEDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After the account exists, copy the Customer ID:

  1. In Google Ads, top-right (near account name/email)
  2. You will see a 10-digit number like 123-456-7890
  3. Add to Railway / .env:

     GOOGLE_ADS_CUSTOMER_ID=1234567890

  (Numbers only without dashes is also fine.)


PART 5 — HOW THIS TIES TO SEO CRM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Step                              Status in your app
  --------------------------------  ------------------------------------------
  Google Ads account + billing      You — this guide
  GOOGLE_ADS_CUSTOMER_ID            Add to Railway env vars
  GOOGLE_ADS_DEVELOPER_TOKEN        Separate request in Google Ads API Center
                                    (can take days to weeks)
  OAuth "Connect Ads" in app        Not live yet — needs developer token first

Order: payment + account now → developer token later → Keyword Planner sync in CRM.


COMMON GOTCHAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Wrong country — card country must match billing country or payment fails.
  2. 3D Secure / bank SMS — EU/UK cards often need extra verification; complete it.
  3. Account created but ads will not run — billing incomplete or campaigns paused
     (OK for Keyword Planner prep).
  4. Accidental spend — pause campaigns: Campaigns → select campaign → Paused.
  5. Manager account (MCC) — for multiple clients later, create at:
     https://ads.google.com/home/tools/manager-accounts/
     Not required for a single site.


MINIMUM CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ ] Google account logged in
  [ ] Ads account created at ads.google.com
  [ ] Billing country + timezone set
  [ ] Payment profile (Individual or Organization) filled
  [ ] Card/payment method added and verified
  [ ] Campaigns PAUSED if you are not advertising yet
  [ ] Customer ID copied → GOOGLE_ADS_CUSTOMER_ID
  [ ] (Later) Apply for developer token in API Center
