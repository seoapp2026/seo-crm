

About the privacy policy and terms

You are right: they are not normal shop/blog privacy policies. They are written for this CRM so Google can see what the tool is, what data it uses, and that Google data is only used for the features you enable (Search Console, Analytics, Ads keyword research), not sold or used to advertise to third parties. Google requires those pages when connecting OAuth / APIs. That is why they live on the CRM domain.

What permissions are needed

Not many, and not endless. Only a small fixed set:

1. CRM login: shared password (and optional allowed emails) to open the app.
2. Google connections (optional): when you connect each service, Google asks consent for:
   - Search Console: read performance data
   - Analytics: read metrics
   - Ads: keyword research data (after developer token approval)
   - Basic email/identity of the connected Google account
3. One-time Google setup: OAuth credentials, privacy/terms URLs, and Ads developer token approval (this last one is what we are waiting on).

These are mostly read permissions for research and dashboards. The CRM does not need permission to publish ads, manage billing, or take over your Google account. You can revoke access later from Google.

Do we need many things of this type?

No. The practical list is: domain/hosting, privacy + terms pages, Google OAuth, user consent when connecting Google, Ads developer token approval, and any API keys you own (for example OpenAI). After that there is no long chain of extra special permissions for normal CRM use.
